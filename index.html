About UKCCU

A community of Christ-like believers growing as a family

Upper Kabete Campus Christian Union (UKCCU) is the premier university Christian union based at Upper Kabete Campus, University of Nairobi. We bring together students from the Faculty of Agriculture and Faculty of Veterinary Medicine for holistic growth, leadership development, mentorship, and campus evangelism.

Our mission is to equip every member through Bible studies, weekly campus fellowships, e-teams, and discipleship for faithful, impact-driven Christian living.
